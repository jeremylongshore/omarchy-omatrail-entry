# Acceptance journeys

## New expedition to trail

1. Open omaTrail from the bar.
2. Name five travelers, select occupation, difficulty, month, and profile.
3. Buy the minimum required oxen, food, ammunition, and clothing.
4. Depart and verify the wagon, party, supplies, date, route, and next stop.

## Trail decisions to ending

1. Travel under multiple weather, pace, ration, and region conditions.
2. Resolve at least one event in every category.
3. Cross each river using available methods and visible conditions.
4. Rest tired travelers and repair a worn wagon with a part.
5. Reach either victory or loss and verify survivors, named losses and reasons,
   duration, difficulty, resources, avoidable waste, score, and replay seed.

## Hunting expedition

1. Start a hunt from trail or landmark state.
2. Move with the keyboard and right-click, aim and fire with the keyboard and
   left-click, pause, toggle assist, and request an early return.
3. Verify moving region-eligible wildlife, ammunition, accuracy, carry limit,
   spoilage, waste, time, and result explanation.
4. Return to the trail and verify exact ammunition, food, day, fatigue, harvest,
   and waste reconciliation.

## Profile and save continuity

1. Switch from Green Monitor to Color Deluxe during a journey and hunt.
2. Verify only presentation changes.
3. Close and reopen omaTrail, then restore the same state.
4. Corrupt the primary save and verify last-good recovery or a clear fresh start.

## River crossing order

1. Reach a river and inspect width, depth, current, weather, and available services.
2. Select an available method and verify no crossing occurs before confirmation.
3. Confirm the order and verify the deterministic result treatment.
4. Enable reduced motion and verify the current-line animation stops without
   hiding the result or explanation.

## Balance distribution

1. Run `npm run test:balance` against the unchanged engine and policy version.
2. Verify exactly 100,000 terminal runs cover all 480 matrix cells across both
   rules profiles.
3. Verify reckless, naive, skilled-heavy, and skilled-light completion rates
   remain inside their Blueprint bands.
4. Verify the exploit policy does not beat either skilled policy on survival and
   does not exceed the better skilled median score by more than 10 percent.
5. Replay a fixed sentinel with one worker and multiple workers and compare the
   canonical aggregate hash.

## Exact-SHA marketplace evidence

1. Run all offline, race, mutation, audit, and vendored gate lanes.
2. Commit a clean tree.
3. Run Buzz validation and capture from that exact commit.
4. Inspect the preview at marketplace scale and approve all three checks.
5. Submit only the receipt-bound SHA after explicit owner approval.
